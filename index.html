Community Impact & Purpose

Why This Webpage Matters to Our Community

Artificial intelligence shouldn't be locked behind expensive hardware, complex software installations, or privacy-invading cloud servers. By bringing our Teachable Machine model directly into a lightweight webpage, we turn advanced computer vision into an everyday tool accessible to anyone with a smartphone or laptop.

100% Private Local Processing

Unlike traditional cloud AI apps that upload your personal photos or live video to external servers, this webpage runs TensorFlow.js directly inside your browser. Your camera frames and uploaded images never leave your device—protecting household, school, and community privacy.

Connected Feature: Browser Edge Execution

Built for Every Device & Workflow

Community members have different needs in the field. Use Live Webcam Detection for hands-free, real-time screening and sorting, or switch to Image Upload Testing to inspect saved photos from mobile devices—even when bandwidth is limited after the initial page load.

Connected Feature: Live Camera & File Upload

Empowering Informed Decisions

Rather than giving an opaque "yes/no" answer, our confidence bars and certainty indicators show users how confident the AI actually is. This transparency builds AI literacy in classrooms, workshops, and local initiatives—helping users verify results before taking action.

Connected Feature: Live Probability Meters

Bridging Classroom & Community Innovation with Open Web Standards

Hosted openly via static web infrastructure (GitHub Pages), this tool requires zero subscriptions, zero API keys, and zero server maintenance. Students, educators, volunteers, and residents can immediately share a single link to test visual classification models, collaborate on datasets, and solve local challenges together.
